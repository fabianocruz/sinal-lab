# Sinal.lab — Product Definition
_Living document — refined every AutoLoop cycle based on user feedback_

## Purpose
Sinal.lab is an AI-native intelligence platform for the LATAM tech ecosystem.
Weekly newsletter + real-time dashboard for CTOs, founders, and VCs.

## Target Audience
- Technical founders and CTOs in Latin America
- VCs and investors focused on LATAM
- Senior engineers evaluating stack decisions

## Content Pillars (by weight)
1. **AI & Infraestrutura Inteligente (35%)** — LLMs, AI agents, infra, governance
2. **Fintech & Infraestrutura Financeira LATAM (30%)** — Open Finance, Pix, stablecoins, embedded finance
3. **Venture Capital & Ecossistema (20%)** — Funding rounds, M&A, VC theses
4. **DevTools & Engenharia (15%)** — Frameworks, open source, developer experience

## Editorial Voice
- Data-driven, not hype-driven
- Opinionated analysis, not summaries
- LATAM angle must be genuine, not forced
- Portuguese (BR), tech terms in English

## Quality Bar
- No press releases disguised as editorial
- No filler (if the analysis says "skip this", don't publish it)
- No sponsored content
- Minimum 3 sources for financial claims
- Never use em dash (use comma, colon, or period)
